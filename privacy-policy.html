Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. What We Collect

QR Scanner does not collect personal data. When you scan a QR code, the image is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to any server.

2. How We Use Data

Scan results are used solely to display the decoded content to you. We do not store, share, or sell scan data.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies or tracking technologies.

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
