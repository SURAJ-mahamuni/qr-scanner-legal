1. Introduction

Welcome to QR Scanner ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles permissions, local device storage, and third-party advertising services when you use the app. By installing or using QR Scanner, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Permissions We Request & How They Are Used

QR Scanner requests only the minimum system permissions required to deliver core scanning and feedback features:

Permission Purpose & Data Handling
Camera
(android.permission.CAMERA)		 Used exclusively via Android CameraX to provide a live viewfinder and analyze QR codes and 1D/2D barcodes in real time. Camera frames are analyzed strictly in volatile memory on your device using the bundled Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning model. No photos, video streams, or camera frames are ever saved, recorded, or transmitted off your device.
Internet & Network State
(INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)		 Used by the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to load banner advertisements and when you explicitly choose to open a scanned web URL in your browser.
Vibration / Haptics
(VIBRATE)		 Used only to provide optional tactile vibration feedback upon a successful scan (toggleable in Settings).

3. On-Device Data Storage (History, Favorites & Generated Codes)

QR Scanner lets you scan and generate QR codes (Text, URLs, vCard Contacts, Email, SMS, Phone Numbers, Wi-Fi credentials, Geo Locations) and 1D barcodes (EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 128, Code 39, ITF-14, Codabar).

  • Local Database Only: If the "Save to history" setting is enabled, your scanned payloads, generated codes, and starred Favorites/Saved items are stored strictly locally on your device inside an internal SQLite (Android Jetpack Room) database. We have no access to this data.
  • App Preferences: Your configuration choices (Theme, Haptic feedback, Beep sound, Auto-copy, After-scan action, and QR dot shape) are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.
  • Export & Sharing: When you export your history to a CSV file or share/save a generated QR/barcode image, the file is created locally on your device and handed off to Android's system Share Sheet or media gallery via FileProvider solely at your direction.

4. System Actions & Intent Hand-offs

When you tap action buttons on a decoded result—such as Save Contact, Make Call, Open Link, or Open Email—the app uses standard Android system Intents (such as ACTION_DIAL, ACTION_INSERT, ACTION_VIEW, or ACTION_SENDTO) to open your device's default phone dialer, contacts app, browser, or email client with the scanned details pre-filled. QR Scanner does not read your existing contacts, call logs, SMS messages, or emails.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

We do not collect personal information directly. However, QR Scanner integrates third-party software development kits (SDKs) provided by Google that may collect certain device and usage data as governed by Google's own privacy policies:

A. Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

We use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements in the app. To serve, measure, and prevent fraud in advertisements, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the following categories of data:

  • Device or Other IDs: Your device's Google Advertising ID (AAID) and app set ID.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse geographic location inferred from your device's IP address.
  • App Activity: Ad impressions, ad clicks, and basic app interaction events.
  • App Info & Performance: Crash logs, diagnostics, and device specifications (OS version, screen size).

All data transmitted by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time on your Android device by navigating to Settings → Google → All services → Ads.

B. Google ML Kit (Bundled Barcode Scanning)

QR Scanner uses the bundled variant of Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning. Barcode recognition runs entirely offline on your device. ML Kit may periodically record basic SDK configuration and performance diagnostics in accordance with Google API policies.

Learn more about how Google handles data at the following official links:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Policies
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services
  • Google ML Kit Terms & Privacy

6. Data Retention & Your Deletion Controls

Because QR Scanner does not maintain user accounts or remote databases, you have complete, immediate control over all data stored by the app:

  • Delete Individual Items: Delete any specific scan or generated code directly from the History screen.
  • Clear All History / Clear Saved: Use the Clear all option in History, Saved, or Settings to permanently wipe records from your device.
  • Disable History Recording: Toggle off Save to history in Settings at any time.
  • Full Erasure: Uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in Android Settings immediately deletes the local database and all preferences.

7. Children's Privacy
